# Gratitude

We're sketching out some ideas around a [Gratitude Journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gratitude_journal) app that runs on the [AT Protocol](https://atproto.com/).  Still early but our idea is to learn about the AT Protocol while making a nice clean app that engages users in regular, positive, life-affirming mental patterns.

# Golden Key Matrix

**A quick self-check for how well your design and development teams work together.**

Great architecture can't fix a bad user experience. The [Golden Key Matrix](https://golden-key-matrix.com/) helps teams align technical architecture with design architecture by rating themselves across three keys:

- **Open Mindset**: curiosity, respect, and treating questions as ideas rather than criticism.
- **Integrated Workflows**: prototype run-throughs, UX intention discussions, dev build explanations, and cross-team pairing.
- **Rich Ecosystem**: prioritizing, choosing the right collaboration mediums, setting cadence, and owning the effort.

Each key is scored on a Design vs. Development quadrant, from Basic to Expert. Use it every sprint or two, and be honest; the value comes from candid answers.

Today it's a reference page you can bring to discussions or print out. Next, we're exploring accounts and progress tracking for individuals and teams.

Born from the talk *[The Golden Key to Badass Products](https://opensourcenorth.com/presentations/The-Golden-Key-to-Badass-Products)* by Dorothy Toth (UX Designer, BI WORLDWIDE) and Adam Dehnel (Platform Engineer, Improving).
